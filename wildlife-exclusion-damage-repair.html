Common signs

Signs You May Need to Compare Exclusion Provider Options

These signs can help homeowners decide whether exclusion or repair-related service categories are relevant.

Visible entry holes

Openings around siding, rooflines, vents, soffits, fascia, or foundation areas.

Damaged vents

Torn, loose, or missing vent covers around attic, dryer, or exterior vent areas.

Soffit or roofline gaps

Loose panels, gaps, chew marks, or vulnerable roofline access areas.

Chimney access concerns

Possible wildlife access near chimney caps, flashing, or exterior openings.

Crawl space openings

Ground-level gaps, loose doors, damaged screens, or repeated activity nearby.

Repeat wildlife activity

Animals returning to the same areas after previous activity was noticed.
