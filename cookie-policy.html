1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, scripts, or analytics identifiers.

These technologies help websites remember preferences, understand general usage patterns, improve performance, and provide a smoother browsing experience.

  • To help maintain website security, performance, and reliability.
  • To improve the experience for homeowners comparing provider options.
